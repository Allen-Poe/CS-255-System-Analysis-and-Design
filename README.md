# CS 255 System Analysis and Design

This repository includes my business requirements document and system design document for the DriverPass projects.

## Project Summary

The client was DriverPass, a business that helps people prepare for their driving tests. They wanted a system where customers could purchase training packages, take online classes and practice tests, and schedule driving lessons. Employees also needed to manage reservations, track customer progress, and control account access.

## What I Did Well

Breaking the client's requests down into specific requirements and showing how different people would use the system. My diagrams covered customer tasks and employee responsibilities, including checking instructor and vehicle availability before booking lessons.

## What I Would Revise

I would improve the layout of my diagrams. Some connections became crowded, especially in the use case diagram. I would give the shapes more space and adjust the lines so someone could follow each connection more easily. Someone without technical experience may have a hard time following the connections.

## Understanding User Needs

I used the interview transcript to identify what customers and employees needed from the system. I turned those needs into requirements and then used diagrams to show how the features would work. For example, the booking process checks package hours and availability before saving a reservation. Considering user needs matters because a system can work correctly and still be frustrating or unhelpful if it does not support what people actually need to do.

## My Approach to Software Design

I start by understanding the problem and breaking it into smaller tasks. For future projects, I would review the requirements with the client, sketch the main processes, and check that the diagrams match all those requirements. I would also walk through any unsuccessful situations that may lead to complications later on in the devlopment cycle.
